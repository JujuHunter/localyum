Internal Notice:
This document is maintained by Local Yum staff. Management review is not required.

Bolle making is a core tradition at Local Yum. It has been practiced by staff members long before standardized procedures were introduced.

For clarity: The Supervisor has no authority over bolle making.

Bolle recipes, techniques, and timing are shared orally between employees. Written documentation is intentionally limited.

Trust, repetition, and care are essential. The dough will tell you when it is ready.

If you have questions about bolle making, ask another employee. Do not escalate.
