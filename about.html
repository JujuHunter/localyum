Our Mission

At Local Yum, we strive to provide more than just beverages and pastries. Our goal is to create a comfortable, familiar experience for every visitor.

Our Values

  • Consistency
  • Quality
  • Community
  • Respect for Tradition

Our History

1996 – Local Yum founded in Vestnes, Norway.
1998 – First pastries added to the menu.
2001 – Introduced seasonal drinks.
2005 – Vestnes location remodeled for modern efficiency.
2010 – Minor expansion of staff and menu.
2015 – Continued tradition of customer-first service.
2020 – Focused on quality and consistency amidst global changes.
2026 – We are still committed to serving our community.

Local Yum may only have a single location, but we believe small spaces allow for big experiences. Visitors often remark on how familiar everything feels from the first visit.

Ask staff about “special orders” -- sometimes they remember more than they say.
