Are you passionate about coffee, pastries, and creating a warm atmosphere? Local Yum is always looking for new members to join our family.

Barista / Server

Responsibilities include:

  • Preparing and serving coffee and beverages
  • Assisting customers with menu selections
  • Maintaining a clean and welcoming environment

Requirements: Friendly attitude, teamwork, and reliability.

Pastry Assistant

Responsibilities include:

  • Helping prepare gluten free pastries and special menu items
  • Stocking and organizing gluten free ingredients
  • Assisting in maintaining gluten free food quality standards

Requirements: Attention to detail, punctuality, and enthusiasm.

  • Opportunities for growth and learning
