Our Regular Client Morissey Gets a Free Drink

Archived article – originally published on the Local Yum website (2000).

At Local Yum, we value our regular customers and the sense of familiarity they bring to our café every day.

This week, our team decided to thank one of our most loyal visitors, Morissey, by offering him a free drink of his choice.


Morissey at his usual table.

Morissey has been visiting Local Yum consistently for years, often sitting in the same spot and ordering the same beverage. Staff members recognize him immediately.

The free drink was offered quietly, without announcement. Morissey accepted with a smile and continued his routine as usual.

Moments like these remind us that Local Yum is more than just a café — it is a shared space built on habits, familiarity, and trust.

Note: No further documentation was recorded for this visit.
