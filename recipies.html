Our "Famous" Cherry Pie

"A slice of home, served at room temperature."

Ingredients:

  • 2 ½ cups All-purpose flour (Sifted twice)
  • 1 cup Unsalted butter (Cold, cubed)
  • 4 cups Fresh cherries (Pitted—DO NOT leave pits in)
  • ¾ cup Granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp Lemon juice
  • A pinch of salt (Not 3%, Morissey!)

Preparation Steps:

  1. The Crust: Mix flour and butter until it looks like "sand." If it looks like "mud," you have failed. Chill for 30 minutes.
  2. The Filling: Simmer cherries with sugar and lemon juice until the red is deep and dark. Like the color of the office carpet.
  3. Assembly: Roll out the dough. Lay it into a 9-inch tin. Pour in the filling.
  4. The Lattice: Weave the top carefully. A broken lattice is a broken promise.
  5. Baking: Bake at 200°C until the crust is golden. Allow to cool. Do not serve while screaming.


Recommended pairing: Black coffee, no sugar.
