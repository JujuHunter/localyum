Accessibility Features at Local Yum

We want every visitor to enjoy Local Yum, whether online or in our café. Here are some of the ways we aim to be accessible:

  • High contrast design for readability.
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation across all pages.
  • Alternative text for images, including our iconic spinning coffee cup.
  • Scalable text for easier reading — adjust your browser zoom to your preference.

If you encounter anything difficult to access, please contact us and let us know!

Thank you for visiting Local Yum - we want everyone to enjoy our pastries, coffee... and much... much more!! ;p
