At Local Yum, every barista practices latte art daily. Each day focuses on a specific pattern to ensure consistency and quality.

Monday Heart
Fundamental pour. Focus on balance and control.
Tuesday Rosetta
Maintain steady hand movement throughout the pour.
Wednesday Tulip
Layering technique. Precision is required.
Thursday Swan
Advanced practice. Optional.
Friday Local bear
Friday is the bear day! Draw the FAMOUS local yum bear for every custommer!
Saturday Customer Request
Adapt techniques to individual preferences.
Sunday Repetition Practice
No evaluation. Review all forms.
